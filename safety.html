Women’s safety

Prepared, not fearful.

Safety is about practical habits, good information and knowing where to turn when something feels wrong.

Digital safety

Use strong, unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, review account recovery options and avoid sharing sensitive information publicly.

Travel & everyday awareness

Tell a trusted person your plans when appropriate, keep your phone charged, know your route and trust your judgment when a situation feels unsafe.

Emergency planning

Keep important contacts accessible and learn the emergency services available in your location. For an immediate threat, contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

Annïka does not provide emergency response or professional legal, medical or security services. Guidance here is general awareness material.
