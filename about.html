About Annïka

Built around a simple idea.

Muslim women deserve digital spaces that feel useful, respectful, calm and genuinely worth returning to.

What we’re building

Annïka is an independent digital concept bringing several everyday needs into one experience: modest fashion inspiration, faith-focused learning, women’s safety awareness and lifestyle resources.

How we approach content

We aim for clear language, practical value and responsible sourcing. Religious references should be verifiable, safety content should avoid overstated promises, and recommendations should be transparent.

Where it can go

The current site is a lightweight static foundation. It can later grow into a full application with accounts, saved resources, community tools and a richer content system.
