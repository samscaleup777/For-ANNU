Image transparency

Image sourcing & rights

Annïka uses product-specific image searches so each catalog item starts with an image query based on its own name, section and use case.

Where imagery comes from

For catalog refinement, Annïka prioritizes openly licensed and public-domain media surfaced through Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, with regional search terms such as Bangladesh, Pakistan, Arab, Saudi Arabia and Gulf where those terms are useful to the product.

What is not copied

Images from Facebook pages, boutiques, marketplaces or other commercial stores are not automatically free to reuse just because they are publicly visible. Those photographs should only be added when the owner has granted permission or the image is otherwise clearly licensed for the intended use.

Verification matters

Openverse aggregates media hosted by third parties and its documentation says developers should independently verify the license for each work. Wikimedia Commons likewise notes that each file can have different licensing and attribution conditions. Annïka therefore keeps source/credit information available for future catalog refreshes rather than treating a search result as blanket permission.

The catalog is a prototype store. Product photos show representative items/styles and are not a claim that Annïka owns or stocks the exact photographed brand or supplier SKU.
