How to Build a Modest Capsule Wardrobe

Fashion · 8 min read · Annïka Editorial

A modest wardrobe becomes easier to manage when every piece has a job. Start with a small base of neutral, comfortable layers and add a few pieces that reflect your personal style. Think in outfits, not individual purchases.

Start with five building blocks

Choose a versatile outer layer, two everyday tops, one comfortable bottom, one occasion-ready piece and a small rotation of hijabs or accessories. Prioritise fit, fabric, climate and how often you realistically wear each item.

SEO takeaway: Focus on repeatable combinations, not constant newness. A capsule works when it reduces decision fatigue and helps you use what you already own.

How to Build a Sustainable Quran Reading Habit

Faith · 6 min read · Annïka Editorial

Consistency usually beats intensity. Choose a manageable daily target—such as a short passage after one established part of your routine—and protect that time from distractions.

Make the habit easier to keep

Keep your Quran accessible, decide when you will read, and note one reflection or action after each session. When you miss a day, restart without treating the break as failure.

10 Everyday Digital Safety Habits for Women

Safety · 7 min read · Annïka Editorial

  1. Use unique passwords for important accounts.
  2. Turn on multi-factor authentication wherever available.
  3. Review recovery email addresses and phone numbers.
  4. Limit what personal details are public on social platforms.
  5. Keep your phone and browser updated.
  6. Review app permissions regularly.
  7. Be cautious with unexpected links and attachments.
  8. Use device lock and encryption features.
  9. Back up important photos and documents securely.
  10. Know how to report, block and recover compromised accounts.

Digital safety is mostly about reducing avoidable exposure and having a recovery plan before something goes wrong.

A Faith-Centred Daily Routine That Is Actually Sustainable

Wellbeing · 6 min read · Annïka Editorial

A useful routine should support your life rather than turn into another source of pressure. Anchor your day around prayer and add small, repeatable blocks for reading, movement, nourishing food, work or study, relationships and sleep.

Use anchors, not perfection

Pick three non-negotiables and let the rest flex with your schedule. A routine that survives busy days is more valuable than a perfect timetable that collapses after one difficult week.
